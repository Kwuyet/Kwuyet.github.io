The problem

The case company sells digital-operations services to SMEs, household businesses and cooperatives. It has one full-time founder, so AI agents have to do the work of finance, risk and partner departments.

The founder is offered a 4.2-billion-VND contract to roll out a cooperative network across 20 provinces. Its gross margin is 24% against a 28% target, it needs a performance bond, and taking it would push cash below the 550-million-VND reserve. The founder has to decide whether to accept and which bank product should cover the gap. The data comes from the competition's simulated data pack: contracts, orders, invoices, bank transactions, cash flow and bank products.

What I delivered

  • Analysed how the company operates and where it breaks when it scales: information processing, liquidity and compliance control.
  • Split the work into five candidate agent roles, then consolidated them into a three-agent pipeline so that each agent owns one decision and hand-offs stay simple.
  • Documented the system with data flow diagrams at level 0 and level 1.
  • Wrote the business rules for gross margin, cash-flow forecast and funding gap.
  • Defined the seven risk rules with severity levels, mandatory actions and founder-approval gates for financing above 300 million VND.
  • Designed the confidence score that tells the founder whether there is enough evidence to act on a bank option.

Agent 1

Data & Finance

Cleans contract, order, invoice and cash-flow data. Calculates list price, real cost, gross margin, closing cash and funding need.

→ finance metrics, missing fields

Agent 2

Risk & Compliance

Checks the metrics against the seven risk rules and sets one overall risk level from the most severe rule triggered.

→ risk level, warnings, approval points

Agent 3

Decision & Partner

Filters bank products, ranks them by total cost of funds and collateral, scores confidence and writes the Decision Card.

→ recommendation, 3 reasons, 1 safeguard

Human in the loop

Founder approval

Financing above 300 million VND, or sending documents to a bank, waits until the founder confirms on the dashboard.

→ approved Decision Card

Numbers in code, judgement in the LLM

Every cash-flow and risk calculation runs in deterministic Python, so the language model never produces a financial figure. GPT reads the results, compares the bank options and writes the Decision Card in JSON that must pass a strict schema: one recommendation, exactly three reasons and one cash-protection condition.

funding_need = max(0, 550M − projected_closing_cash)
confidence = 0.4 × eligibility + 0.6 × data_completeness

If cash turns negative and no bank product qualifies, the code overrides the model and stops the deal.

Built for change and for privacy

Crisis cards. The founder types a change in plain Vietnamese, for example "deliver 10 days earlier, costs up 15%". An extraction agent turns it into structured data, the pipeline recalculates, and the dashboard shows the decision before and after.

Data masking. Customer names, phone numbers and tax codes are replaced with tokens before any data reaches the OpenAI API. They are restored only inside the company's own environment, and files go to a bank only after the founder approves.

Risk rules I defined

RR-001Bank transaction with a risk score of 85 or more. CRITICAL Freeze and have the founder verify.
RR-002Closing cash after the deal falls below 550M VND. HIGH Propose working-capital financing or split the contract into phases.
RR-003Gross margin below the 28% target. MEDIUM Cut costs or renegotiate scope.
RR-004Credit or contract files would be sent to an outside partner. HIGH Founder must allow the release.
RR-005Loan or guarantee above 300M VND. HIGH Mandatory founder approval gate.
RR-006Cash below the reserve and confidence score under 0.65. CRITICAL No recommendation until data is completed.
RR-007Delivery expected to slip by more than 7 days. MEDIUM Assess penalty risk and plan extra capacity.

The company, customers and banks in the case are simulated competition data. Diagrams are in Vietnamese, the language of the submission.

The problem

Booking a cinema seat touches many rules at once. Two customers cannot hold the same seat, a showtime cannot overlap another in the same room, and a voucher only applies to the right audience and order size. Payment then has to issue tickets, points and receipts in one step.

The team had to specify all of this for two actors (Customer and Admin) and four external systems: payment gateways (VNPAY, MoMo, ZaloPay), email/SMS, and the QR scanner at the cinema door.

What I delivered

  • Wrote the seven business process descriptions and the problem statement that the team's requirements were built on.
  • Modelled UC04 Select showtime & seats and UC12 Manage cinemas & seat maps, with their activity diagrams.
  • Drew the sequence diagram for updating a member profile.
  • Identified the domain classes, drew the class diagram and grouped it into five packages.
  • Led the data design (16 classes to a normalized relational model with keys and indexes) and the deployment architecture. Recommended SQL Server after comparing it with MySQL and PostgreSQL.
  • Coordinated nine people as team lead.

Business rules captured in the spec

Seat holdSelected seats are locked for 10 minutes while the customer pays. Maximum 8 seats per booking.
Showtime clashA new showtime is rejected if it overlaps another in the same room; end time is calculated from film length.
PromotionA code is checked against validity dates, target audience and minimum order value before the total is recalculated.
E-ticketEach seat gets its own QR code, sent by email and SMS and stored in the booking history.
LoyaltyPoints are added after every successful payment; members move up tiers (U22, Silver, Gold) automatically.
RefundCustomers can cancel a booking themselves (UC08) within the refund policy.

The problem

At many Vietnamese research units, patent applications are handled half by hand in Word and Excel. Granted patents live somewhere else, fees and valuations are not recorded, and choosing an International Patent Classification (IPC) code depends on one person's experience.

The database covers seven entities: User, Application, Application history, IPC code, IP right, Fee and Right economics.

The application lifecycle we modelled

Submitted→ Under examination⇄ More info requested→ Accepted/ Rejected

Every state change is written to an audit history, and an accepted application becomes an IP right with a certificate number, protection term, owner and economic value.

What I delivered as team lead

  • Wrote the project outline the team worked from and drove the write-up.
  • Split the work across five members, set deadlines and ran four review meetings, from scoping the entities to the final test run.

Rules enforced in the database

  • Trigger logs every status change with old state, new state, time and note.
  • Trigger creates the IP right automatically when an application is accepted.
  • Trigger blocks deleting a user who still owns an active right.
  • Trigger rejects fees that push an application over its allowed total.
  • Two roles: applicants can only read catalogue tables; admins have full control.

Contract expiry alert (my view)

-- Contracts ending in the next 30 days, ranked by urgency
CREATE OR ALTER VIEW v_HopDong_SapHetHan AS
SELECT hd.MAHD, nv.MANV, nv.HOTEN, pb.TENPHONG,
       hd.LOAIHD, hd.NGAYKT,
       DATEDIFF(DAY, GETDATE(), hd.NGAYKT) AS ConLai_Ngay,
       CASE
         WHEN DATEDIFF(DAY, GETDATE(), hd.NGAYKT) <= 7  THEN N'KHẨN CẤP'
         WHEN DATEDIFF(DAY, GETDATE(), hd.NGAYKT) <= 15 THEN N'CẢNH BÁO CAO'
         ELSE N'Cảnh báo sớm'
       END AS MucDo
FROM HOPDONG hd
JOIN NHANVIEN nv ON hd.MANV = nv.MANV
JOIN PHONGBAN pb ON nv.MAPHONG = pb.MAPHONG
WHERE hd.TRANGTHAI = N'Có hiệu lực'
  AND hd.NGAYKT BETWEEN GETDATE() AND DATEADD(DAY, 30, GETDATE());

Three urgency levels (7, 15 and 30 days) so HR can act on the most urgent renewals first.
